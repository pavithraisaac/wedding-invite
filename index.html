Getting here

Travel Notes

By Train

Chennai Central · About 3 km to the cathedral and 3 km to Lite Auditorium. Main hub for trains from Bangalore, Hyderabad, Thiruvananthapuram and Coimbatore.

Egmore · About 5 km to both venues. Main hub for trains from Madurai, Tiruchirappalli, Tirunelveli and Nagercoil.

By Road

St. George's Cathedral is beside the Gemini / Anna flyover, near the US Consulate and Semmozhi Poonga.

Lite Auditorium is very close to Kilpauk Medical College.
